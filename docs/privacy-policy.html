Baby Vax — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Baby Vax is a child vaccination schedule tracker. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, where it is stored, and how to delete it. It is written to be read, not to cover anyone legally.

The short version. Baby Vax stores the child details you type in, plus your email address, in your own private area of Google Firebase. Nobody else can read them. There are no ads, no analytics profiles, no tracking across other apps, and nothing is sold or shared with advertisers. You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

Who is responsible

Baby Vax is made by Ahmed Al-Assar, an independent developer. For anything in this policy, contact ahmedassr360@gmail.com.

What the app collects

DataWhyWhen
Email address To create your account and sign you in. At sign-up
Password To sign you in. It is handled by Firebase Authentication and stored only as a cryptographic hash. The developer never sees it. At sign-up
Child's name and date of birth The date of birth is what generates the vaccination schedule. The name is used to personalise the app and the reminders. At sign-up
Growth details: height, weight, head size, blood percentage Shown back to you in the app so you can keep track. They are not analysed or sent anywhere else. At sign-up, and whenever you edit them
Vaccination schedule and its status The generated appointment dates, and whether you marked each one completed. While you use the app
Crash diagnostics If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics records the error, the stack trace, the device model and the OS version, so the bug can be fixed. This does not include your child's details. Only when a crash happens

What the app does not collect

  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • No advertising identifier, and no ads of any kind.
  • No behavioural analytics and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No push notification tokens. Vaccination reminders are scheduled entirely on your own device and work with no internet connection. No reminder is ever sent from a server, so there is no device token to store.

Where your data is stored

Your account is held in Firebase Authentication and your child's details in Cloud Firestore, both run by Google Cloud. Crash reports go to Firebase Crashlytics. Google processes this data on the developer's behalf; see Firebase's privacy information.

A copy is also cached on your own device so the app works offline.

Who can see it

Only you. The database security rules allow a signed-in user to read and write only the records belonging to their own account; every other request is refused by the server, not just hidden by the app. The developer does not read individual users' child data.

Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or data brokers. It would only ever be disclosed if a valid legal order required it.

How long it is kept

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account it is removed immediately, as described below. Crash reports are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days and are not linked to your child's details.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Delete account, confirm with your password, and the app will permanently delete:

  • your child's profile and all growth details,
  • the entire vaccination schedule,
  • all scheduled reminders on the device,
  • your sign-in account itself.

This cannot be undone, and there is no separate "deactivate" state. Full instructions, including how to request deletion if you can no longer open the app, are on the account deletion page.

Children

Baby Vax is designed for parents and carers, who must be 18 or over. It is not directed at children and children are not meant to use it. The data it holds is about a child, entered by their parent.

Your rights

You can see all of your data inside the app, correct it by editing your profile, and delete all of it from Settings. If you would like a copy of your data in a file, or have any other request, email ahmedassr360@gmail.com.

Security

Traffic between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit. Passwords are never stored in readable form. Server-side security rules restrict every record to its owner, and Firebase App Check is used to reject requests that do not come from a genuine installation of the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it, and significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.

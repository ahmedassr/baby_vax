### Full description (≤ 4000 characters)

```
Baby Vax keeps your child's vaccination schedule in one place and reminds you before every appointment.

Enter your child's date of birth once. Baby Vax builds the full immunization schedule from birth through to the teenage booster, and reminds you the evening before each appointment and again on the morning of the day.

WORKS WITHOUT INTERNET
Reminders are scheduled on your own phone, not sent from a server. They arrive on time whether or not you have a connection, and your schedule stays readable offline. This matters if your connection is unreliable.

WHAT YOU GET
• A complete schedule generated from your child's date of birth
• A reminder the evening before, and on the morning of, each appointment
• Mark each dose as completed and see what is still due at a glance
• See the next upcoming vaccination as soon as you open the app
• Keep track of height, weight, head size and blood percentage
• Health tips for each stage
• Full Arabic and English, including right-to-left layout
• Light and dark themes

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your child's details are stored in your own private area and are readable only by you. There are no ads, no tracking across other apps, and nothing is sold or shared with advertisers. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, at any time, in a few taps.

A NOTE ON THE SCHEDULE
Baby Vax is an organising tool, not medical advice. The schedule is based on the Palestinian Ministry of Health routine childhood immunization schedule, which follows the World Health Organization's EPI recommendations. National schedules differ between countries and change over time, so always follow your own doctor and your country's national schedule.

Baby Vax is for parents and carers aged 18 and over.

Questions or problems: ahmedassr360@gmail.com
```

## Data safety form

Play asks this question by question. The answers below match what the app
actually does after this release.

**Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** → **Yes**

**Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?** → **Yes**

**Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?** →
**Yes**, with the deletion URL (the hosted `delete-account.html`).

### Data types to declare

| Category | Data type | Collected | Shared | Processed ephemerally | Required | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Personal info | Email address | Yes | No | No | Required | Account management |
| Personal info | Name (the child's) | Yes | No | No | Required | App functionality, Personalisation |
| Personal info | Other info (date of birth) | Yes | No | No | Required | App functionality |
| Health and fitness | Health info (height, weight, head size, blood percentage) | Yes | No | No | Optional | App functionality |
| App activity | Other actions (vaccination completion status) | Yes | No | No | Required | App functionality |
| App info and performance | Crash logs | Yes | No | No | Optional | Diagnostics |
| App info and performance | Diagnostics | Yes | No | No | Optional | Diagnostics |

### Explicitly answer "No" to

- Location (approximate or precise)
- Financial info
- Messages, Photos and videos, Audio, Files and docs, Calendar, Contacts
- Web browsing history
- **Device or other IDs** — the app stores no push token or advertising ID
- Personal info → User IDs is **not** declared: the Firebase UID is an internal
  account key, not shared or used for tracking

### Security practices

- Data is encrypted in transit: **Yes**
- Users can request data deletion: **Yes**
- Committed to the Play Families Policy: **No** (the app targets 18+)
- Independent security review: **No**

> If you later re-enable Firebase Cloud Messaging for console campaigns, you
> must add **Device or other IDs → collected** to this form and mention the
> token in the privacy policy. It was deliberately removed, so today the answer
> is No.

## Target audience and content

- **Target age group:** **18 and over, only.** Do not tick any age band below
  18.
- **Appeal to children:** No. The app is a scheduling tool for parents; the
  branding is not child-directed.
- This keeps Baby Vax **out of the Google Play Families programme**, which is
  the intent — Families brings a stricter review and extra requirements the app
  does not need.

  account already has a full schedule."

## Release checklist for the first upload

1. Build the signed AAB (commands in `RELEASE_NOTES_FOR_AHMED.md`).
2. Create the app in Play Console with the package name above.
3. Fill in this listing, upload the icon, feature graphic and screenshots.
4. Complete Data safety, Content rating, Target audience, Health declaration,
   Ads and App access.
5. Add the hosted privacy policy URL and the account deletion URL.
6. Upload the AAB to a **closed test** track.
7. Recruit **at least 12 testers** and keep them opted in for **14 consecutive
   days** — this is required before you can apply for production access.
8. Apply for production access, then roll out.

Delete your Baby Vax account

App: Baby Vax (com.ahmedalassar.babyvax) · Last updated: 6 November 2026

You can delete your Baby Vax account and everything in it from inside the app. You do not need to contact anyone or wait for approval.

How to delete it in the app

  1. Open Baby Vax and sign in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Read the warning, type your password to confirm it is you, and tap Delete permanently.
This cannot be undone. There is no grace period and no way to restore the data afterwards. If you only want to stop using the app for a while, sign out instead.

What gets deleted

Everything, immediately:

  • your sign-in account and email address,
  • your child's name, date of birth, height, weight, head size and blood percentage,
  • the entire generated vaccination schedule, including which doses you marked as completed,
  • every vaccination reminder scheduled on your device,
  • the copy of your data cached on your device.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Crash diagnostics — if the app ever crashed, Firebase Crashlytics holds the error and device model for up to 90 days. These records are not linked to your child's details and expire automatically.
  • Nothing else is retained. There are no backups kept after deletion.

If you cannot open the app

If you have uninstalled Baby Vax, lost access to your device, or cannot sign in, email ahmedassr360@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, and ask for your account to be deleted.

Requests are handled within 30 days. You may be asked to confirm from the registered email address, so that nobody else can delete your data.
